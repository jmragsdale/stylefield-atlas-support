Stylefield Atlas support

Browse art-style references and prepare prompts. This app does not generate images or include generation credits. ChatGPT and Midjourney are separate services with their own accounts, terms and any fees. The app is independently developed and is not affiliated with either service.

Find a reference

Open a collection and search names, codes, descriptions or tags; use category filters to narrow it. Open a style for its four samples and prompt builder. Tap a sample for the full-screen viewer, swipe between samples, and pinch or double-tap to zoom. Close the viewer to return. The heart saves or removes a favorite. Favorites and Recents appear on Home when they contain styles.

Build a prompt

Choose ChatGPT or Midjourney. Enter a subject for a new image, then copy or share the prepared prompt. For ChatGPT photo restyling, choose Restyle attached image and a strength, then attach your photo directly in ChatGPT. Copy 3-way comparison prepares instructions for Subtle, Balanced and Bold versions; the app does not create those images.

Settings and results

Settings contains templates, aspect ratio and Midjourney parameters. Defaults do not automatically match every sample's generation version. External model versions, style references and settings can change results; the bundled examples are not a promise of identical output.

Back up or move favorites

In Settings, choose Export favorites and save the JSON file somewhere you control. Export is disabled with no favorites. Transfer that file to another device, choose Import favorites there, and select it. Recognized style IDs are added; existing favorites remain. Unknown IDs are ignored. Unsupported versions, malformed JSON, files over 1 MiB and lists over 10,000 entries are rejected.

What a backup includes

Favorites backups contain a format version and style IDs only. They exclude photos, subjects, templates, settings and recents. Keep a copy before clearing favorites or deleting app data. Delete exported files separately when you no longer need them.

Manage local information

Settings provides Clear favorites, Clear recents and Clear recent subjects. Clear Default subject separately to remove its current value. Reset template and Reset restyle template restore their built-in text.

Get help

Email jermaine.ragsdale@me.com. Include the app version, iOS version, affected collection/style and steps to reproduce. Share private images or prompt content only if you intend to send them. Email support in Settings opens a draft and does not send automatically. No response-time guarantee is offered.

Requirements

iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. Reference browsing, local favorites and prompt assembly work offline. Opening an external service requires a connection and any account that service requires. Read the privacy policy for app and support-site data details.
