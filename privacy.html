Privacy policy for Stylefield Atlas

Operator: Jermaine Ragsdale. Effective date: October 24, 2026. Privacy contact: jermaine.ragsdale@me.com.

What the app stores

Favorites, recently viewed styles, the current and recent subjects, prompt templates and prompt settings are stored in local app preferences. The app has no app account, advertising or analytics SDK, or developer-operated network backend. It does not access your photo library, camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Actions you choose

Copy places selected text on the system clipboard. Open in ChatGPT sends the generated prompt, including your subject, to chatgpt.com in a URL, which may enter browser history. Open in Midjourney opens its website. Those services have their own terms, privacy practices and any applicable fees. The app does not generate images or upload a photo; you attach any photo directly in the external service. Sharing sends the item you choose to your selected system-share destination.

Favorites files

Export creates a JSON file with a format version and saved style IDs. It excludes subjects, templates, settings, personal photos and recents. You choose the file destination. Import reads your chosen file and merges recognized style IDs into existing favorites.

Control and removal

Settings can clear favorites, recently viewed styles and recent subjects. Clear Default subject to remove its current value; reset the templates to remove custom template text. Deleting app data removes local preferences through iOS. Previously exported files, system backups, clipboard contents and shared copies are managed separately by you, iOS and your chosen providers. Clearing favorites does not delete exported files.

Support email

Email support opens a draft in your mail app; nothing is sent automatically. If you choose to send it, your email address, message and attachments are delivered to jermaine.ragsdale@me.com so the developer can respond to your support or privacy question. Your email service and the support mailbox provider handle the message under their respective policies and settings. The app does not automatically delete support email or control provider backups. Avoid sending sensitive images or prompt text unless you intend to share them.

Support website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages contain no forms, advertising, analytics scripts or remote fonts. When you open these pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub login, under its own privacy statement: GitHub Privacy Statement .

Questions and changes

Contact jermaine.ragsdale@me.com with privacy questions. The effective date above identifies this version of the policy.
